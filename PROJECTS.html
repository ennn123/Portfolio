The Smart Dog Feeder

IoT-Enabled Automated Dog Feeding Systems

The Smart Dog Feeder is an IoT-enabled system designed to help pet owners feed their dogs remotely. It utilizes a Node MCU 1.0 and a servo motor for dispensing food to connect to a web-based control interface. Through the website, users can dispense food on demand, ensuring their pets are fed on time. The system also includes features like feeding history logs and pet profile to make it user friendly. This project offers a practical solution for busy pet owners, promoting consistent nutrition and overall well-being for their dogs.

Automation in Poultry:

A Smart Egg Weighing and Separator System

A system that helps small scale egg farmers to weigh and separate eggs automatically. With the use of Cheap And Low-Cost Components like Arduino Uno, LCD, Servo Motors and Sensors In poultry farms' age of continuous growth and adaptation to technological advancement, the system can act as a foundation for future improvement and innovation. If implemented properly and adequately maintained, the Smart Egg Weighing and Separating System will have great potential for significant contribution to sustainable and efficient poultry farm operations.
